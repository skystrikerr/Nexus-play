# NexusPlay v1.0.0-beta 🎮

**Track your games. Rank everything.**

NexusPlay is a free game tracker: your library, backlog, tier lists, and a release calendar in one place.

👉 **Try it in your browser: https://nexusplay-r4w6.onrender.com** (no download needed)

---

## What's in it

- **Game library** — search 500,000+ games with cover art and add them in a click
- **Tier list maker** — drag and drop your games into S through F tiers
- **Release calendar** — see what's launching over the next three months and wishlist it instantly
- **Play session tracking** — built-in timers that log your hours per game
- **Backlog & wishlist** — plan what to play next
- **Statistics & analytics** — completion rate, hours played, library breakdown
- **Community** — posts, reviews, and public profiles
- **Steam & Xbox sync** — pull in your playtime automatically
- **Guest mode** — try everything without making an account

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `NexusPlay-1.0.0-portable.exe` | Just double-click and run — no installation |
| `NexusPlay-Setup-1.0.0.exe` | Standard installer with Start Menu and desktop shortcuts |

**On phones:** open the site in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) or "Install app" (Android) to get it as a proper app icon.

> Windows will show an "unknown publisher" warning on first run — click **More info → Run anyway**. This is normal for apps without a paid code-signing certificate.

## Known issues

- The server sleeps after 15 minutes of inactivity, so the first visit after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to load (free hosting tier).
- Password reset emails are limited during beta while email delivery is being finished.
- The desktop app requires an internet connection — it connects to the live service.
